# Spartan Society — Year 12 Ancient History lessons

HTML lessons for NSW HSC Ancient History, Section II Option G: *Spartan society to the Battle of Leuctra 371 BC*.

Each lesson works two ways:

- **Present** (projector): `→` or space steps through the slide, `←` goes back, `K` timer, `O` all slides, `T` light/dark, `L` and `1`–`7` colour codes.
- **Read** (phone or revision): press `R`, or open the page on a small screen. Every slide becomes one scrolling page with the notes filled in. Filter to **Notes** or **HSC answers**. Model answers stay hidden until you choose to show them.
